Common questions

Good to know before you begin.

Not sure what to ask for? Here are a few useful starting points. The team can help you understand the next step for your property.

Should I request a survey before drilling?

Yes. Start by discussing your site and water requirements with the team so the appropriate survey and next steps can be considered before drilling begins.

Do you assist with irrigation system planning?

Speroni lists drip and overhead irrigation design, installation and advisory services. Share your crop, field size and water source when you enquire.

Can I request a quotation for my farm or plot?

Yes. Use the quotation form and include your location, intended water use and approximate property or field size so the team has a useful starting brief.

Which areas do you serve?

The Bulawayo poster advertises service across Matabeleland North and Matabeleland South. Contact the team to confirm arrangements for your specific location.

What if I am not sure which service I need?

Select “Not sure yet — need advice” in the form and describe the water challenge you are trying to solve. The team can help you identify the most relevant enquiry.
